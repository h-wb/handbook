# Personal tasks

The one list of what you have to do outside the build. Private. The [assistant](Assistant.md) reads it, proposes the day's focus and files what you tell it.

Each morning it proposes up to three focus tasks and marks them High once you confirm, without giving them a date. The weekly review suggests old tasks to park in Someday, and only your `park <number>` moves one. Its [routines](https://github.com/h-wb/home-ops/tree/main/kubernetes/apps/main/llm/hermes/app/routines) in home-ops say how.

The build's tasks are not here: they are on [the board](Board.md).
