# dawarich-regression

Private: its contents are location data and are not described here.

What it does: compares the location history kept by the location service with its backups, reports days that were lost or damaged, and holds the procedures that repair them. Its jobs run on the job runner on a schedule. It also holds the description of the workflow that turns visited places into notes.

Only agents of the private scope work in it.
