# The road

## Goal

A second brain: something that knows what is going on, so you can spend your time well and be present.

Done when the last milestone below is met. Each step is worth having on its own, so stopping after any of them is fine. When a milestone is met, stop and use it for two weeks before starting the next.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Done when | Stands | The work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Foundation | Scopes, gateways, workshops and an assistant you can talk to | Done | |
| 1. The record | Every source loads by itself for two weeks without anyone touching it | In progress | HOMEL-2 |
| 2. Look back | You can scrub through a day and search what was on screen, on the phone and the desktop | In progress | HOMEL-1 |
| 3. The plan | Your recurring things and project blocks are in the calendar, and one task list is reachable by everyone | In progress | HOMEL-45, HOMEL-69 |
| 4. The right moment | Two weeks of prompts from the assistant, within a daily budget, with a log of which you acted on | Not started | HOMEL-43 |
| 5. It learns | A slipped task is noticed and repaired without you saying so, and the weekly review proposes rule changes | Not started | HOMEL-43 |

Milestone 3 comes before 4 on purpose: without a plan in the calendar there is no free moment to find, and without one task list there is nothing to propose.

## The shape

The systems that carry each milestone: the [warehouse](../Systems/Warehouse.md) for 1 and 2, the [calendar](../Systems/Calendar.md) and the [board](../Systems/Board.md) for 3, the [assistant](../Systems/Assistant.md) for 4 and 5. How the assistant gets from running on a clock to knowing the right moment: [The aware assistant](The%20aware%20assistant.md). How the systems connect: [the map](../Map.md).

## The work

What is in progress, what is next and what is open are on the board and nowhere else: each milestone names its task above. At most three outcomes are in progress at once.
