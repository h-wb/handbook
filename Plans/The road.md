# The road

## Goal

A second brain: something that knows what is going on, so you can spend your time well and be present.

Done when the last milestone below is met. Each step is worth having on its own, so stopping after any of them is fine. When a milestone is met, stop and use it for two weeks before starting the next.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Done when | Stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Foundation | Scopes, gateways, workshops and an assistant you can talk to | Done |
| 1. The record | Every source loads by itself for two weeks without anyone touching it | Nearly. The always-on Mac is left |
| 2. Look back | You can scrub through a day and search what was on screen, on the phone and the desktop | Screen and browser work. Conversations are being built |
| 3. The plan | Your recurring things and project blocks are in the calendar, and one task list is reachable by everyone | The board is reachable by agents. The calendar is not filled in |
| 4. The right moment | Two weeks of prompts from the assistant, within a daily budget, with a log of which you acted on | Not started |
| 5. It learns | A slipped task is noticed and repaired without you saying so, and the weekly review proposes rule changes | Not started |

Milestone 3 comes before 4 on purpose: without a plan in the calendar there is no free moment to find, and without one task list there is nothing to propose.
